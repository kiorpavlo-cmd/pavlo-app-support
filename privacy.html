Privacy
Policy.

This policy explains how Pavlo Kior handles information when you visit the app-support website or use an app I develop. The features you use determine which parts apply. Fat Alien-specific practices are described below.

01

Who is responsible

Pavlo Kior is responsible for personal information that I receive directly, for example when you contact me for support. You can reach me at kiorpavlo@gmail.com.

02

Information handled by the support website

The support site is static. When hosted by GitHub Pages, GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security. The site has no account, advertising, analytics tools, or cookies that I have added. If you submit the support form, your email address, the app you select, and your message (plus your name if provided) are sent through FormSubmit to my support inbox so I can reply. FormSubmit processes the submission under its own privacy terms and retains submissions for up to 30 days. I use the received message to answer your request.

03

Fat Alien: information and device permissions

Depending on the features you choose, Fat Alien stores information on your device such as your account identifier and sign-in provider (Apple or Google), email address if provided by that provider, profile details (for example name or nickname, date of birth, gender, height and avatar), food diary entries, nutrition values, custom foods and recipes, goals, weight and body-fat measurements, and app preferences.

If you enable iCloud sync, app records are synced to your personal private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Apple operates iCloud and handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. The app can also use HealthKit with your permission: it can read active and basal energy and body weight/body-fat values, and write weight and body-fat values to Health. HealthKit access is optional. App data may be synced to your iCloud database when sync is enabled; I do not operate a separate health-data server.

When you look up a product by barcode, Fat Alien sends that barcode to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. That request also carries normal network information, such as your IP address, to the service. The app does not include your sign-in details or food diary in that lookup. Nutrition-label text recognition is processed on your device; label images are not sent to my server by this feature.

04

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, save and restore your app data, synchronize data when you enable iCloud sync, perform barcode lookups, and respond to support requests. The Fat Alien source reviewed for this policy does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. I do not sell personal information.

05

Service providers and other recipients

Information is shared only with services needed for the feature you choose: Apple for Sign in with Apple, iCloud/CloudKit and HealthKit; Google when you choose Google sign-in; Open Food Facts for barcode searches; GitHub Pages to host this website; and FormSubmit to process support-form submissions and forward them to my support inbox. These providers handle information under their own privacy terms. I do not give your app information to advertisers.

06

Storage and retention

App information is stored locally on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, synced records are stored in your private CloudKit database. FormSubmit retains submissions for up to 30 days; I keep support correspondence for as long as needed to answer your request and for reasonable record-keeping or legal requirements. GitHub Pages handles website technical logs under its own practices.

07

Your choices, access and deletion

You can decline optional camera, HealthKit or iCloud permissions and can turn off iCloud sync in the app. You can contact me to ask about information I may hold from support correspondence or to exercise privacy rights available where you live.

The current Fat Alien app provides sign-out and data export, but does not provide a complete in-app account and associated CloudKit-data deletion flow. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete synced iCloud records. To ask for help with a deletion request, email me; removing data stored in iCloud may also require steps in Apple’s account or iCloud controls. This limitation is stated here so the policy accurately reflects the current app.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

08

Children, changes and contact

Fat Alien may ask for a date of birth to personalize nutrition goals. A parent or guardian who has a privacy concern about a child’s information can contact me. I may update this policy when app features or data practices change; the date above shows the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, contact kiorpavlo@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or unnecessary sensitive information.
